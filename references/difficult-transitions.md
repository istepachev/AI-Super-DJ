# Difficult transition toolkit

The goal is a continuous musical handover: retain a useful rhythmic/energetic foundation while the next piece becomes audible. A nominal thirty-second fade can still contain an abrupt switch or an energy hole. Gain measurements and listening remain separate evidence.

## Ordinary execution

`make`/`render` retain ordinary mixing and its existing gradual band/percussion alternatives first. A successful route is returned without new foreground processing. On a qualifying actual energy, foreground or rhythm-coverage refusal, the renderer may invoke `superdj.difficult_mix.recover` on the original, already timed decks. A new sustained-loss check also catches a middle-of-transition reduction exceeding 9 dB relative to available source energy for 1.5 seconds. Real quiet breaks in both sources remain quiet.

Recovery selects at most two foreground recipes and obtains one new percussion estimate pair for those exact raw decks. This is different audio from an earlier vocal-suppressed estimate; never reuse a nonmatching stem to avoid a model call. If neither supported recipe works, retain the failure and the existing bounded native source-window search. Do not add a retry loop around it.

The recipe can retain the outgoing track as the leader and introduce the next as instrumental support, or transfer foreground earlier. Quiet estimate gaps guide the old vocal release. The incoming main voice has one continuous decibel opening, reaching approximately -3 dB at its selected entry and opening gently to unity. No per-word/phrase activity gate repeatedly releases to full volume. These are estimated gaps, not recognized lyric boundaries.

Residual accompaniment can still contain screams and guitar harmonics after separation. Recovery separately softens the supporting residual middle/high bands (up to 12/15 dB) and leaves the low band and estimated drums available for the independently gradual transfer. It does not claim to remove every vocal or repair dissonant chords. It retains native endpoint samples and reconstructs actual components for the front audit. The vocal audit replays the recorded recovery gains, not the ordinary fixed-fraction vocal curves.

Require actual observed fronts on both decks, source/local rhythm coverage, actual energy, estimated-foreground checks and continuity. Source time, pitch, overlap duration, sync tolerance and resource/storage limits remain binding. A failed or missing observation does not become success because a gain curve is smooth. Unavailable estimates, insufficient memory or absent suitable gaps retain refusal.

Per-plan preferences, or a per-edge override:

```json
{"mixing_preferences": {"difficult_transition": {"enabled": true, "foreground_priority": "auto"}}}
```

`foreground_priority` supports `auto`, `outgoing` and `incoming`. Auto tries outgoing first. This controls fallback preference only; it does not force recovery on an ordinary passing mix. `difficult_transition: false` disables this recovery. Explicit band/handoff controls and a selected non-auto middle mode are never replaced by it.

## Conditional preparation and revision tools

Use these SDK tools only after a recorded actual refusal or listener rejection. Supply `TransitionFailure` with the real quality evidence; a successful mix or a generic error is not a reason to enter them. They return candidates, not final audio certification. The skill can apply them during a difficult custom rebuild; ordinary `make` does not automatically pitch-shift, extend riffs or change approved overlap geometry.

- **Another source phrase:** use the existing bounded recovery/index search first. Preserve source locks and complete lyric phrases; choose a strong outgoing phrase before its native fade and an incoming phrase with a usable rhythmic foundation. Retain rejected complete processing contexts; do not rerender the same failed combination under a new temporary name.
- **More overlap:** `transition_tools.longer_overlap(plan, records, failure, bars=...)` submits one deliberately longer candidate to the ordinary arranger, up to 64 bars, preserving order, unrelated boundaries, explicit locks and playback/chapter caps. It never selects a shorter overlap. Keep the same outer compute budget. The appropriate span can be seconds or many bars; do not force a long collision where no musical support exists.
- **Riff introduction/support:** `transition_tools.extend_riff(streams, sample_rate, ...)` works on a bounded selected native excerpt with mixture, voice and drum streams. Select the actual characteristic riff, not an unrelated quiet opening. Use the same edit on all streams. At most four copies and sixty added seconds; protected material is preserved. Native harmony, active bass coherence, level, actual seam energy, low estimated voice and the unchanged 5% seam-interval bound must pass. Actual intra-phrase attack offsets are copied unchanged. The measured phase origin can precede the splice boundary; never move every event onto a uniformly rescaled grid. The result includes mapped beat times and the exact edit geometry. Rebase downbeats, feature/probability profiles and cues with that same edit once, and construct fresh actual source templates/clock evidence before rendering. This is an excerpt tool, not a replacement for the reversible short-track body-loop workflow. Do not use it merely to inflate solo-time percentages. If no clean riff exists, select another technique.
- **Instrumental support with the native verse:** `transition_tools.instrumental_support(...)` keeps the new voice suppressed during the overlap and restores it gently inside a measured quiet gap immediately afterwards. The next native word and all subsequent source PCM retain their original level. A busy or missing gap is refused, rather than releasing a partly sung word suddenly. This supports keeping the strong outgoing phrase as the leader without requiring a riff loop.
- **Whole-source pitch:** `transition_tools.pitch_preparation(...)` deliberately shifts the entire prepared source by one or two semitones, identically for mixture/voice/drums, using an independent formant-preserving pitch-only R3 stage. The ordinary timing stage follows separately. Call `retarget_pitch_record(record, prepared)` to redirect fresh native timing references to the changed streams. Both adjacent transitions must agree on the source pitch; reject a second conflicting assignment instead of hiding a pitch glide. Reanalyse harmonic compatibility and construct fresh source templates; a successful command alone does not establish chord compatibility. This is conditional tonal repair, never the ordinary sync correction.
- **Local contribution repair:** `transition_tools.patch_contribution(...)` changes only a known own contribution with smooth margins, leaving samples outside the chosen interval bit-identical. Supply exact original/new contribution PCM from the render recipe. Never infer an exact own contribution by subtracting an estimated stem from an unknown master. Recheck the actual patched overlap for rhythm, energy, vocal competition and endpoint continuity. Retain other listener-approved material.

All these edits preserve originals. Do not stack speculative changes: first select a small indexed set using native signal evidence, then render only supported candidates. A timeline, pitch or foreground change invalidates its old templates/cache identity and all affected audio checks. Store generated data only in the configured working root and its one total byte budget. Pitch-only preparation checks the working budget before creating its folder; riff and rescue buffers respect available-memory admission. No personal paths, recordings or private reports belong in the public repository.

## Validation and delivery

For a successful ordinary route, compare decoded PCM against the previous engine. For a changed fallback, bit equality is not the objective: it changes the music intentionally. Check reconstructed own contributions, actual native attacks and local coverage, sustained energy, vocal/foreground competition, smooth gain progression, source/solo/chapter durations, pitch consistency across both neighbours, full-set mastering and the independent decoded export comparison. Retain rejected diagnostics. A short audition or signal proxy cannot guarantee subjective quality for every source; ask for listening review when actual musical ambiguity remains.
